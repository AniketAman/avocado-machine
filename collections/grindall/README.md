# Grindall

Snapshot of all 169 problems shown by [Tech Interview Handbook's Grind 75](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/grind75/?hours=40&weeks=4&grouping=topics) at 4 weeks and 40 hours per week, viewed on 2026-09-29. Entries follow the site's topic-grouped order. Each title includes its topic and difficulty. `source.tsv` records the source identity and order.

The 105 problems already present in Blind 75 or Grind 75 directly reference their original exercise, sharing its files and review schedule. The other 64 have local descriptions, TypeScript starters, and focused tests. Their starter solutions are intentionally unfinished.

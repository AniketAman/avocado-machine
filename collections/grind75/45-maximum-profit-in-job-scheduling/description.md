# Maximum Profit in Job Scheduling (Hard)

**Topic:** Binary Search · **Difficulty:** Hard

Choose nonoverlapping jobs to maximize total profit.

## TypeScript interface

`jobScheduling(startTime: number[], endTime: number[], profit: number[]): number`

## Example

`start=[1,2,3,3], end=[3,4,5,6], profit=[50,10,40,70] → 120`

## Rules

A job may start exactly when another ends. The three arrays describe corresponding jobs.

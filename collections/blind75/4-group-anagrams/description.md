# Group Anagrams (Medium)

**Topic:** Arrays & Hashing · **Difficulty:** Medium

Partition the strings into groups whose members are anagrams of one another.

## TypeScript interface

`groupAnagrams(strs: string[]): string[][]`

## Example

`strs = ["act", "cat", "hat"] → [["act", "cat"], ["hat"]]`

## Rules

Groups and members may be returned in any order.

Source: [NeetCode problem](https://neetcode.io/problems/anagram-groups/)

# Meeting Rooms

**Topic:** Intervals · **Difficulty:** Easy

Decide whether one person can attend every meeting interval without overlap.

## TypeScript interface

`canAttendMeetings(intervals: number[][]): boolean`

## Example

`intervals = [[0, 30], [5, 10]] → false`

## Rules

A meeting ending when another begins does not conflict.

Source: [NeetCode problem](https://neetcode.io/problems/meeting-schedule/)

# Longest Repeating Character Replacement (Medium)

**Topic:** Sliding Window · **Difficulty:** Medium

Find the longest substring that can become one repeated uppercase letter after at most k replacements.

## TypeScript interface

`characterReplacement(s: string, k: number): number`

## Example

`s = "XYYX", k = 2 → 4`

## Rules

Replacements may choose any uppercase English letter.

Source: [NeetCode problem](https://neetcode.io/problems/longest-repeating-substring-with-replacement/)

# Meeting Rooms II (Medium)

**Topic:** Intervals · **Difficulty:** Medium

Find the minimum number of rooms needed for all meeting intervals.

## TypeScript interface

`minMeetingRooms(intervals: number[][]): number`

## Example

`intervals = [[0, 40], [5, 10], [15, 20]] → 2`

## Rules

A room becomes free at a meeting’s end time.

Source: [NeetCode problem](https://neetcode.io/problems/meeting-schedule-ii/)

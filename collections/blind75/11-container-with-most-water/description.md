# Container With Most Water (Medium)

**Topic:** Two Pointers · **Difficulty:** Medium

Choose two vertical bars and maximize the water area between them.

## TypeScript interface

`maxArea(heights: number[]): number`

## Example

`heights = [1, 7, 2, 5, 4, 7, 3, 6] → 36`

## Rules

Area equals index distance times the shorter height.

Source: [NeetCode problem](https://neetcode.io/problems/max-water-container/)

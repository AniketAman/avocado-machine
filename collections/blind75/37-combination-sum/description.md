# Combination Sum

**Topic:** Backtracking · **Difficulty:** Medium

Find every unique multiset of the distinct positive numbers that sums to target.

## TypeScript interface

`combinationSum(nums: number[], target: number): number[][]`

## Example

`nums = [2, 5, 6, 9], target = 9 → [[2, 2, 5], [9]]`

## Rules

A number may be used repeatedly. Combination and member order do not matter.

Source: [NeetCode problem](https://neetcode.io/problems/combination-target-sum/)
